Official Amazon description

Faharia by Lauren James Li

FAHARIA
Some laws preserve peace. Others bury the truth.

For centuries, the Oath of Poter has kept the peace.

No Fae magic shall remain strong in the presence of humans.
No Fae shall use magic to touch a human soul.
No Fae shall kill a human.

Aryvida has spent her life protecting the Oath.

Until the day she breaks it.

When a prince from the human kingdom crosses into Faharia, one act of mercy shatters an ancient covenant. Forgotten truths awaken. Old enemies stir. And a war long buried begins to breathe again.

Drawn together by a secret neither of them understands, Aryvida and the prince must decide whether peace is worth the lies upon which it was built.

Because once the Oath begins to crack...

There is no putting the world back together.

Perfect for readers who love:

  • Epic fantasy
  • Slow-burn romance
  • Fae mythology
  • Ancient magic
  • Political intrigue
  • Rich worldbuilding
  • Hidden kingdoms
  • Morally complex characters
